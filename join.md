<div class="page-section">
  <h2 class="page-section-title">
    <span data-lang="ko">가입 절차</span>
    <span data-lang="en">How to Join</span>
  </h2>

  <div class="join-steps">
    <div class="join-step">
      <div class="join-step-num mono">01</div>
      <div>
        <strong><span data-lang="ko">문의 이메일 발송</span><span data-lang="en">Send an inquiry email</span></strong>
        <p><span data-lang="ko">성명, 소속, 직위, 연구 분야를 포함하여 아래 이메일로 문의해 주세요.</span>
           <span data-lang="en">Please email us with your name, affiliation, position, and research area.</span></p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="join-step">
      <div class="join-step-num mono">02</div>
      <div>
        <strong><span data-lang="ko">가입 신청서 작성</span><span data-lang="en">Complete the application form</span></strong>
        <p><span data-lang="ko">학회 사무국에서 가입 신청서를 안내해 드립니다.</span>
           <span data-lang="en">The society office will provide you with an application form.</span></p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="join-step">
      <div class="join-step-num mono">03</div>
      <div>
        <strong><span data-lang="ko">심사 및 승인</span><span data-lang="en">Review and approval</span></strong>
        <p><span data-lang="ko">이사회 심사 후 가입 승인이 통보됩니다.</span>
           <span data-lang="en">You will be notified of approval following review by the board.</span></p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="join-step">
      <div class="join-step-num mono">04</div>
      <div>
        <strong><span data-lang="ko">회비 납부</span><span data-lang="en">Pay membership dues</span></strong>
        <p><span data-lang="ko">연회비 납부 후 회원 자격이 발효됩니다. (회비 금액은 창립총회 후 공지 예정)</span>
           <span data-lang="en">Membership becomes effective upon payment of annual dues. (Dues amount to be announced after the inaugural assembly)</span></p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
